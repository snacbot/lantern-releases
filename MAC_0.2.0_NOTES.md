## More comfort for your Mac

A more comfortable Mac preview, with pointer recovery and better compact-window settings.

**[Download for Mac ↗](https://github.com/snacbot/lantern-releases/releases/download/mac-v0.2.0/Lantern.dmg)** &nbsp; · &nbsp; Public preview &nbsp; · &nbsp; macOS 13+ · Apple Silicon & Intel

Open the DMG, drag Lantern to Applications, then open Lantern.

---

### In this preview

- Shake your mouse or trackpad pointer back and forth several times to restore this Mac's screens to 100%, including software dimming. Enabled by default; turn it off in Settings > General > Shake mouse to restore brightness.
- General settings remain usable in compact windows.
- LAN control defaults on for new installations and older settings without a saved choice; saved opt-outs are preserved. Settings > Network includes Scan LAN.
- Installation reports send automatically once without a popup.

Existing settings, presets, pairing data, login preferences and required automatic updates are preserved. Use Lantern on a trusted local network and the same connection port on each computer.

This remains a public preview. macOS 13 and Intel are build targets; physical sleep/wake, hotplug, fullscreen/Spaces and another-Mac acceptance remain incomplete. Software dimming darkens screen content without switching off the physical backlight.
